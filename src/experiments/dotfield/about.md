Columns stand on a grid, all one height and one width, so from above each is a
dot: its top. A breeze leans them, gusts roll across in bands and eddies turn
them across the wind, and each column swings back on a spring of its own. The
wind is smooth, so neighbours lean together while the field as a whole moves
in waves. Colour belongs to the stalks and wanders slowly across the field.
