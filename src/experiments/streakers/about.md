A row of emitters stands just off screen. Each lets go of a dot every so often,
at random, and every dot it has let go of travels away in a straight line at
that emitter's one speed. Nothing draws the line: it exists only as the dots
on it, and since no two gaps are the same it is plainly there and never fixed.
